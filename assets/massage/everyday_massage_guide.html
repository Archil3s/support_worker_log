Achilles cross-friction — not up and down

Pinch your Achilles between thumb and forefinger. Move across it side-to-side rather than along its length. This cross-fibre friction technique, backed by research on tendon remodelling, breaks down adhesions and improves collagen fibre organisation. The Achilles is a tendon — it needs different treatment than a muscle.

1

Natural pop rule

If a joint makes a small pop during slow, comfortable movement, do not panic and do not repeat the move to chase more pops. The aim is comfort and easier movement, not the sound.

Okay only if there is no pain, numbness, dizziness, weakness, or symptoms travelling down an arm or leg.

2

No neck, jaw, or spine cracking

Do not twist, pull, jerk, or thrust the neck, jaw, ribs, back, hips, or pelvis to make them crack. Those techniques need clinical training and screening.

Use massage, heat, breathing, positioning, and the gentle stretches instead.

For a tight neck or back, guide the stretch slowly to the first comfortable tension, hold, then ease out. Pair it with slow breathing and light pressure around the muscle, not the joint.
